# Scenario 01 — Login, Navigation & Dashboard

## Story

It's your first day. IT has given you a username and a temporary password for OpenBoxes, the system your warehouse uses to track every box, pallet, and shipment that comes in or out. Before you touch any real inventory, you need to be able to log in, find your way around, and understand what the dashboard is telling you.

## Learning objectives

- Log in and select the correct location/depot.
- Identify the main navigation areas and the module relevant to your role.
- Read and personalize the dashboard.
- Know where to find in-app and online help.

## Roles / permissions

Any role. Every OpenBoxes user, regardless of function, starts here.

## Prerequisites

- A username and password provided by your administrator (Scenario 04 covers how these are created).
- Access to at least one location that has been assigned to your user account.

## Walkthrough

1. Navigate to your organization's OpenBoxes URL.
2. Enter the username and password given to you by your administrator and select **Login**.
3. If your account has access to more than one facility, OpenBoxes will prompt you to choose a **location** (depot). Locations are organized by country office and location group — pick the warehouse or facility you actually work in. You can switch locations later from the top navigation if you have access to more than one.
4. You land on **My Dashboard**, your personal home page for that location. This is where you'll return after most tasks.
5. Explore the left navigation panel. Note the main modules you'll use most:
   - **Inbound** — incoming purchase orders and shipments, receiving.
   - **Outbound** — stock movements/shipments you are sending out, picking, packing.
   - **Inventory** — browse on-hand stock, bin locations, cycle counts, adjustments.
   - **Stock Lists** — recurring replenishment lists for facilities you supply.
   - **Configuration** — locations, products, users (visible only if your role grants access).
6. Switch between the preset dashboard views: **Warehouse Management** (current stock, incoming/outgoing shipments, in-progress transactions), **Inventory Management** (accuracy/quality indicators), **Transaction Management** (historical stock movement/shipment data), and **Fill Rate** (how well requests are fulfilled).
7. Personalize **My Dashboard**: drag an indicator to reposition it, drag one to the archive to remove it, and re-add an archived indicator if you change your mind. Save your layout.
8. Locate the help resources: the **?**/help links inside the app, and the external knowledge base at https://help.openboxes.com.

## Checkpoints

- [ ] You are logged in and looking at the correct location's dashboard (confirm the location name shown matches your assigned depot).
- [ ] You can name which left-nav module you'd use to receive a shipment, and which one you'd use to check how much stock is on hand.
- [ ] Your dashboard layout has been changed and saved at least once (proves you found the drag/save controls).

## Common mistakes

- Selecting the wrong location at login and then wondering why expected inventory/documents "aren't there" — always check the location name shown before you start work.
- Confusing **Inbound** (things arriving at your location) with **Outbound** (things leaving your location) — this trips up almost everyone in week one.
- Assuming the dashboard is the only place to see data — it's a summary; the underlying lists (Inbound movements, Outbound movements, Inventory browse) always have the full detail.

## Discussion questions

- If you support more than one facility, how would you check you're looking at the right one before making a change?
- Which of the four dashboard views is most useful for *your* specific job, and why?
